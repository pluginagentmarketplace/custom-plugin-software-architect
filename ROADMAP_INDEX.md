## Competency Framework Overview

### Four Core Dimensions

#### 1. Database Systems
Foundational understanding of database architecture, storage models, and platform capabilities.

**Coverage Across Roles:**
- PostgreSQL DBA: EXPERT focus
- MongoDB: EXPERT focus
- Redis: EXPERT focus
- SQL Developer: ADVANCED focus
- Blockchain Engineer: ADVANCED focus
- Product Manager: ADVANCED focus

#### 2. Query Optimization
Techniques for writing, analyzing, and optimizing data access patterns and performance.

**Coverage Across Roles:**
- PostgreSQL DBA: EXPERT focus
- MongoDB: EXPERT focus
- Redis: ADVANCED focus (specialized in cache/pipeline optimization)
- SQL Developer: EXPERT focus
- Blockchain Engineer: ADVANCED focus (gas/storage optimization)
- Product Manager: INTERMEDIATE focus (SLA/cost focus)

#### 3. Data Modeling
Design patterns and techniques for structuring data efficiently.

**Coverage Across Roles:**
- PostgreSQL DBA: ADVANCED focus (schema design)
- MongoDB: ADVANCED focus (document patterns)
- Redis: ADVANCED focus (cache patterns)
- SQL Developer: EXPERT focus (normalization and design)
- Blockchain Engineer: ADVANCED focus (on-chain state design)
- Product Manager: ADVANCED focus (product/business perspective)

#### 4. Management Skills
Operational and strategic responsibilities for database systems and organizations.

**Coverage Across Roles:**
- PostgreSQL DBA: ADVANCED focus (ops and HA)
- MongoDB: ADVANCED focus (ops and replication)
- Redis: ADVANCED focus (ops and persistence)
- SQL Developer: INTERMEDIATE focus (basic ops)
- Blockchain Engineer: INTERMEDIATE focus (auditing/compliance)
- Product Manager: EXPERT focus (strategic/stakeholder focus)

## Key Concepts and Frameworks

### Proficiency Levels
- **Expert:** 5+ years, architect solutions, mentor capability
- **Advanced:** 3-5 years, independent problem-solving, architectural contribution
- **Intermediate:** 1-3 years, guided execution, pattern following
- **Strategic:** 5+ years, business-focused, organization impact

### Assessment Criteria
**Technical Proficiency**
- Schema design and optimization
- Execution plan understanding
- Performance troubleshooting
- Replication and HA knowledge

**Operational Excellence**
- Monitoring and alerting
- Backup and recovery
- Maintenance operations
- Incident response

**Strategic Thinking**
- Scalability planning
- Technology evaluation
- Cost and resource management
- Business alignment
